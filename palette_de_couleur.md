## Brand & Style
The design system powers an intelligent civic-tech ecosystem bridging citizens, municipal coordinators, and sanitation crews in rapidly expanding central African urban landscapes such as Douala and Yaoundé. The visual identity reflects civic pride, community accountability, and modern environmental stewardship. 

The aesthetic is Modern African Civic-Tech: an intersection of hyper-legible utility, structured tactile components, and energetic organic resonance. The UI feels robust, dependable, and immediately decipherable under high-glare equatorial sunlight. It rejects visual clutter in favor of high-contrast micro-surfaces, tactile push-states, and unambiguous visual statuses. The tone is encouraging, authoritative without being intimidating, and engineered to empower diverse users—from municipal managers tracking truck routes to citizens submitting geo-tagged reports or sorting compostable waste.

## Colors
The color architecture is built to withstand high-brightness outdoor use while preserving intuitive civic meaning:

- **Primary (`#0D7A48`)**: Deep Equatorial Emerald. Anchors the identity, representing urban health, sustainability, and primary interactive elements. It carries high contrast ratios against light backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#E06B14`)**: Solar Ochre / Civic Amber. Denotes urgent action items, alerts, attention, and real-time field incident reports.
- **Tertiary (`#4D7C0F`)**: Leaf / Biomass Olive. Represents sorting streams, organic/valorized waste categories, compost programs, and secondary confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#1E2421`)**: Iron Soil. A deep, organic-tinted charcoal black delivering crisp typography and deep contrasts without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Functional Semantic Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#F9FAF7` (Crisp bone with subtle warm earthen undertone to prevent eye fatigue under outdoor glare).
- **Surface Pure**: `#FFFFFF` (Elevated card containers, lists, and sheets).
- **Status Tiers**:
  - *Signalé (Reported)*: Soft Amber Container (`#FEF3C7`) with Solar Ochre Ink (`#B45309`).
  - *Affecté (Assigned)*: Cyan Teal Container (`#E0F2FE`) with Deep Slate Blue Ink (`#0369A1`).
  - *En cours (In Progress)*: Warm Maize Container (`#FEF9C3`) with Raw Umber Ink (`#854D0E`).
  - *Résolu (Resolved)*: Emerald Container (`#DCFCE7`) with Deep Forest Ink (`#15803D`).
  - *Valorisé (Recovered/Upcycled)*: Fresh Leaf Container (`#ECFCCB`) with Deep Olive Ink (`#3F6212`).

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the progressive, technical geometric cadence of **Space Grotesk** for headlines, telemetry figures, and structural badges with the warmth and legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, instructions, and reports.

Space Grotesk brings an urban infrastructure rigor, ensuring that numbers, weights, metrics (e.g., "12.4 Tonnes", "Quartier Akwa"), and status tags read clearly at a glance. Plus Jakarta Sans maintains an open counter structure and balanced x-height, rendering French municipal terminology clearly even on low-cost mobile displays under bright outdoor conditions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an adaptive fluid grid model built around responsive margins and consistent 4px/8px modular units:

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer margins and `12px` gutters. Critical actions occupy bottom thumb zones with fixed sticky anchors.
- **Tablet & In-Vehicle Terminals (600px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters. Two-pane views decouple incoming citizen incidents from map tracking overlays.
- **Desktop & Supervisor Central (>1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at `1280px` maximum width with `32px` gutters, accommodating multi-stream dispatch views and municipal analytical matrices.

Touch targets across all interactive mobile surfaces enforce a minimum boundary of 48px to accommodate one-handed operation in the field by workers wearing gloves or citizens on the move.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids excessive blur effects, which wash out in bright daylight. Instead, it relies on **tonal containment, structured border definition, and warm, tactile cast shadows**.

1. **Flat Base (Level 0)**: Canvas background `#F9FAF7`. Non-interactive structural zones.
2. **Surface Layer (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards bounded by a crisp 1px neutral hairline border (`#E4E8E3`). Low-elevation ambient shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 36, 33, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating & Tactile Layer (Level 2)**: Action sheets, modal drawers, and operational summaries. Outlined with `#D1D9D0` and backed by a grounded drop shadow: `0 8px 20px -3px rgba(30, 36, 33, 0.12)`.
4. **Active/Drag Layer (Level 3)**: Truck route tracking nodes, floating action buttons (FABs), and emergency waste hazard alerts. Distinctive high-contrast shadow: `0 14px 28px -4px rgba(13, 122, 72, 0.22)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides a modern civic aesthetic that balances friendliness with structural clarity. 

- Interactive buttons and inputs use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radiuses to feel defined and tactile.
- Information cards, telemetry dashboards, and camera capture viewports utilize `1rem` (`16px`) radiuses for organic containment.
- Status badges, category pills, chips, and quick-filter buttons adopt full pill radii (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against square content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Button**: Filled Emerald (`#0D7A48`) with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `Space Grotesk 600`. Height: 52px on mobile for easy thumb-reach. Border radius: `8px`. Active state yields a tactile 1.5px downward translation with a deepened background (`#085632`).
- **Urgent Action / Alert Button**: Filled Solar Ochre (`#E06B14`) with white text. Reserved for reporting hazards, uncollected overflowing waste, and immediate dispatch requests.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: White surface with a 1.5px border in `#0D7A48` and primary emerald text. Used for secondary navigation and dismissals.

### Status Pills & Badges
All status elements are compact, uppercase, and pill-shaped with dedicated foreground and background pairings:
- **Signalé**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, with a subtle warning beacon icon.
- **Affecté**: Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`, showing the assigned collector vehicle ID.
- **En cours**: Background `#FEF9C3`, text `#854D0E`, accompanied by a progress spinner indicator.
- **Résolu**: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`, displaying a verified checkmark icon.
- **Valorisé**: Background `#ECFCCB`, text `#3F6212`, carrying the closed-loop recovery symbol (leaf/recycle).

### Cards
- **Incident / Sector Card**: `#FFFFFF` background, 16px radius, bordered by `#E4E8E3`. Header includes neighborhood metadata (e.g., "Douala IV – Bonabéri"), timestamp, status pill on the right, and embedded geo-coordinates. Bottom section features quick operational actions: "Prendre en charge" or "Voir itinéraire".
- **Metric Metric / Tonnage Card**: Features a high-contrast accent left edge (4px solid `#0D7A48` or `#4D7C0F`), highlighting metrics like daily collected weight or active community sorting points.

### Form Inputs & Dropdowns
- **Input Fields**: 50px height, `#FFFFFF` background, framed by 1.5px `#D1D9D0` borders. Text set in `Plus Jakarta Sans 14px`. Focused state switches to a 2px outline in Primary Emerald (`#0D7A48`) with a soft glow (`rgba(13, 122, 72, 0.15)`).
- **Photo Upload Container**: A dashed 2px outline (`#0D7A48`) featuring a tactile camera button in the center, designed for quick geotagged evidence capture in poor connectivity scenarios.

### Waste Stream Chips (Sorting & Recovery)
- Horizontal scrollable filter chips (`Plastique PET`, `Biodéchets`, `Métal`, `Verre`, `Carton`). 
- Default state: `#FFFFFF` background, `#1E2421` text, 1px `#D1D9D0` border.
- Selected state: Primary Emerald (`#0D7A48`) background, white text, with an inline micro-counter displaying collected volume.

### Lists
- Dividers are subtle `#EEF1ED` hairline rules. Each list row maintains a minimum height of 64px, pairing an iconography slot (category icon with tinted circular background) on the left and a status pill or distance metric ("120 m") on the right.
